## Research questions and possible contributions

1. **Opportunity:** Do the saved WMT prefixes contain a useful quality–cost trade-off that existing stopping rules missed?
2. **Failure localization:** How do generated prefixes differ from prefixes encoding a known target, and what does this comparison establish about representation, decoding and generation?
3. **Recognizability:** If opportunity exists, can a causal controller recognize when further refinement is worth its incremental cost?
4. **Intervention:** If useful early answers are absent, can one specifically motivated training or decoding change create them?

A stronger paper may deliver an adaptive improvement, a carefully bounded negative result explaining the bottleneck, or both. Software completeness and additional experiments alone are not success criteria. Do not make positive results a requirement for finishing a phase.

**Decision rule:**

- **Headroom supported:** A feasible development assignment meets the quality/diagnostic margins and offers at least 5% estimated mean savings versus full depth after plausible monitoring costs, with positive estimated improvement versus the feasible fixed comparator. This permits a bounded controller study; it is not a speedup claim.
- **Headroom unsupported:** Predeclared search finds no such assignment. Close controller development for this cycle unless a bounded amendment addresses a specific limitation. Report “not found by this search,” unless a valid global bound supports a stronger conclusion.
- **Inconclusive:** Cost estimates, optimizer gaps or quality uncertainty prevent a defensible decision. State the missing evidence and price a specific next measurement before requesting it.
