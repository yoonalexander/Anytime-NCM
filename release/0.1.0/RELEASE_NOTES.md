# Anytime NCM — research snapshot 0.1.0

A verified research and engineering closeout for frozen-NCM adaptive stopping. **The original combined quality-and-latency advantage was not established.**

## What is useful

- A dependency-free Python inspector: `anytime-ncm status` and `python -m anytime_ncm status --json` expose source-linked aggregate findings offline.
- A buildable wheel/source distribution, runnable example and clean-install acceptance checks.
- The portable historical Phase 1-5 viewer, with 2,992 recorded trajectories and saved matching-state Concept Firewall forecasts. It does not generate new predictions or include the later campaign.
- A compact research evidence/portfolio bundle with the outcome, case study, figures, provenance and checksums.

## Research outcome

The final frozen controller saved **5.076% actual mean ready-output time** on 1,188 development-evaluation inputs, but **0.664892 BLEU loss exceeded 0.5 allowed**. The conditional paired timing interval is [3.377%, 6.861%]; it does not establish independent quality assurance. Other quality points passed their margins, not confidence bounds. Fixed-depth frontier and original full comparison matrix remain unestablished/incomplete.

Q0 remains failed; reserved E3/E4/unallocated and 7,700 proposed-confirmation outcomes remain sealed. Original missing process/resource receipts, failures and conservative charges remain preserved. No experiments were rerun for delivery. This is not a translation service, quality guarantee or journal publication.

## Verification and rights

Software checks use tiny CPU fixtures with neural weights/corpora/GPU/network blocked, apart from pinned text-tokenizer metadata for one decoder check. Distribution checks exercise fresh wheel and source installs outside the checkout and both entry points. Release downloads are verified against `SHA256SUMS` in the post-publication delivery receipt.

Read `RIGHTS.md` and `NOTICE.md`: the upstream MIT notice is retained, but no repository-wide license for original project material has been invented. Models, private corpora and activations are not bundled. The viewer is the already-public historical artifact.
