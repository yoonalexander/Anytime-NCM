# Saved terminal-cost screen: no further budget for the same earliest-exact rule

October 24, 2026. The [source-locked procedure](../../../docs/studies/evidence-gaps/e2-terminal-screen-protocol.md) was pushed at `598364c45863285e8187b902ffd8fa314147fd27` before this calculation. **This is post hoc development analysis:** E2 outcomes and fixed-method aggregate timings were already known when the question was chosen. It does not replace [E2's negative gate](../e2/summary.md) or constitute fresh confirmation.

For each of the same256 E2 audit identities, take its earliest exact-final-text level and assemble that level's already measured terminal-only request in each of two process blocks/two repeats. No earlier inspections or decision computation are charged. The assembled constituent requests are real measurements, but their aggregation is not an integrated online selector benchmark, new experiment, certified minimum-latency assignment or global opportunity bound.

No E3/E4 release or fits follow this screen.
