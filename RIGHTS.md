# Rights and artifact availability

This repository does not currently grant a repository-wide license for original project code, documents or figures. This release preserves that status; public availability is not an additional license grant. A future licensing decision belongs to the rights holder.

The separately maintained Next-Concept Modeling source is pinned in `third_party/ncm` and carries its own MIT license and copyright notice. Its license does not automatically license this entire research repository. The upstream notice is retained in `NOTICE.md` and the installed package's `upstream-NCM-LICENSE.txt`.

Model checkpoints, corpora, private trajectories and full activations are not included in Python distributions or the compact research snapshot. Their own access and redistribution terms apply. The historical HTML viewer is already retained in the public repository and contains previously released examples; its contents are evidence from those original studies, not newly licensed training data.

The manuscripts are historical internal drafts with their stated authorship, rights and independent-review limitations. A GitHub research release is not a journal submission or independent scientific endorsement.
