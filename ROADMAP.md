## Completion boundary

The research ambition—useful quality-preserving adaptive acceleration—was not achieved. Software/reconstruction/installation validation is separate from scientific effectiveness. No more model fitting, threshold selection, collection or confirmation is automatically authorized. Original failures, missing terminal observations, conservative charges, cohorts and margins remain intact.

The exact previous long-form roadmap is preserved in the [pre-delivery archive](docs/history/pre-delivery-03e3e70/manifest.json) and Git commit `03e3e7086988db46c2979410b5d3081d24f1d340`. Its historical test totals are not current test claims. Independent human review, manuscript rights/authorship and publication remain separate human decisions.
