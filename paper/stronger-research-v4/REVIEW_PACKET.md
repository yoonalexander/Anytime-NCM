## Questions for an independent researcher

1. Does the focused contribution adequately distinguish early-output availability, causal recognition and net execution cost? Is any negative statement broader than its actual checkpoint/rule/population?
2. Is E2's exact-text rule correctly interpreted as a clairvoyant diagnostic with structural relative-quality equality? Could the description mislead a reader into believing that future full text is available to a real controller, or that the assignment is a certified latency optimum?
3. Are the59-family adjusted intervals, rank guard, conservative cue envelopes, two-process resampling and unknown document dependence represented honestly? Are any claimed guarantees stronger than the assumptions support?
4. Are original S1 selection uncertainty and surrogate costs separated from later actual request measurements? Does the post hoc terminal assembly remain visibly distinct from confirmation, a new online benchmark and a global bound?
5. Are strong fixed-depth comparisons and quality-loss margins sufficient for the stated practical question? Do sparse number/negation coverage and the prospective removal of nonidentical rules limit the inference as clearly as intended?
6. Which additional data, if any, would change a scientific decision rather than merely add experiments? Are deferring target encoding/intervention and closing the same earliest-exact budget justified by the evidence?
7. Is the bounded negative result a useful and sufficiently focused contribution for public dissemination? Which claims/figures should be narrowed or removed? This packet supplies no assurance about novelty or venue acceptance.
