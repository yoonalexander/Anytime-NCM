## Statistics and decisions

No candidate fitting/reoptimization. One parity-checked quality output per method/identity; repeats are timing observations. Corpus BLEU/chrF use SacreBLEU2.6.0 exact summed sufficient statistics with direct corpus-score parity and recorded signatures. Literal numeric F1 averages reference-number-covered identities; literal negation mismatch averages all identities. These are text proxies, not factuality/safety. Original margins: BLEU loss0.5, chrF loss1.0, numeric-F1 loss0.02, negation-mismatch increase0.02.

Prospectively reduced simultaneous family: `12 methods*4 quality endpoints + 1 exact rule*11 fixed cost contrasts` = **59 two-sided intervals**, including redundant structural/full-self quality contrasts without reallocating their alpha. Bonferroni tail `.05/(2*59)`; no post-outcome reduction. Exactly 100,000 shared PCG64 identity resamples, seed20261026, generated as int32 `(100000,256)` indices; then from that same generator generate int32 `(100000,2)` process indices. Resamples retain all methods together, average the two repeats within identity/process first, then recompute equal-weight identity/process ratio of means. Process-specific point reductions are descriptive sensitivities; two process sessions give limited process-population information.

Corpus BLEU/chrF differences use the paired resamples, not sentence-score averages. Numeric/negation differences use E0's signed Clopper-Pearson envelope: `[-U(number_negative,n,alpha), U(number_positive,n,alpha)]` where U is the one-sided binomial upper bound. No covered numeric identities -> unresolved unless structural equality applies. Exact/full-self structural cue differences are identically zero by construction. Finite-sample equality alone for other fixed depths must use the conservative envelope. The lock records this family's zero-event minimum coverage; n256 cannot certify a nonidentical rule at the 0.02 margin under this method. Neither undefined coverage nor degenerate bootstrap alone is a pass.

For every resampling interval use E0's Monte Carlo rank guard: `k=binom.ppf(.001/(2*59),100000,tail)`; lower sorted draw k, upper sorted draw B-k+1 (one-based). k=0 means unresolved. The extra .001 bounds numerical tail-rank uncertainty, not sampling-model error. Approximate bootstrap sampling coverage assumes adequate input-group resampling; unknown document dependence remains unsolved. The exact rule's quality equality is structural; fixed-depth intervals and timing still carry these limitations. All draws and intervals are reported, never winner-selected.

A quality endpoint passes only if its adjusted lower difference >= negative margin (BLEU/chrF/numeric), or upper negation difference <=0.02. A fixed depth is excluded as infeasible only if an adjusted interval lies wholly beyond a quality tolerance; otherwise it remains potentially feasible, including sparse-cue/unresolved cases. Full depth is always included. Exact headroom passes only with >=5% point mean reduction over terminal11, positive adjusted lower cost bound, and positive adjusted lower reduction versus **every** potentially feasible fixed depth. If full-relative adjusted upper bound is below5%, report bounded exact-rule no-go. Otherwise failure to pass is inconclusive. Never convert failure into absence of all useful early outputs or a bound on all possible assignments/schedules.
