# Population and document-aware inference design after exploratory Q1

October 25, 2026. **Design complete; empirical launch remains blocked.** The user accepted the recommendation to design an eligible population and document-aware inference after [exploratory Q1](q1-exploratory-results.md). This is documentation and deterministic planning arithmetic only. It does not authorize acquisition, reference inspection, tokenization, synthetic simulations, Q2 measurements, controller fitting or reserved-output access. The original Q0 failures and Q1 selection history remain unchanged.

## Decision and immediate next work

Use a separately reserved German-to-English news population, with complete source-document/alias components assigned to development or confirmation before outcomes. WMT newstest2015 through newstest2020 are a **candidate frame**, not verified eligible data. Their official task pages were inspected; archives, rows, references, document counts, usable licenses and checkpoint overlap were not inspected or verified. No corpus files were downloaded. Changing the corpus changes the population of the claim: a result on this frame would not confirm a WMT14-specific population result or generalize to arbitrary translation.

A larger sentence count alone does not repair document dependence. The conservative cluster cue construction below has a valid conditional argument, but is far too loose under the stated maximum component size and input ceiling. Do not replace it with an all-zero bootstrap bound. The recommended next executable unit is a **bounded source/reference-metadata eligibility audit**, under a separate committed lock, paired with an analytical screen of whether any prospectively justified cluster method can meet the retained criteria. No model output is needed for that audit. If metadata or method feasibility fails, retain a no-go; do not acquire more years or run Q2 automatically.

## Paired inference and preservation of input weights

The intended independent sampling unit is a complete document/alias component. All candidate/full/fixed observations for an input travel together. For a component c, retain its summed corpus statistics, input count n_c, numeric-covered count m_c, paired cue sums and paired timing totals. Headline cue estimates are sum loss/sum applicable count. A document containing20 eligible inputs contributes20 inputs, not the same endpoint weight as a one-input document.

Prospective paired corpus/cost construction: resample complete components with replacement, using the same component multiplicities across methods and all endpoints; sum their sufficient statistics and recompute corpus BLEU/chrF and ratio-of-total cue/cost estimates. Resample process/session blocks with the same block indices across methods, crossed with component draws; average within-input repeats before resampling. Session repeats do not create new input clusters. Retain year diagnostics and a predeclared leave-one-year-out sensitivity; these are not an opportunity to choose a favorable analysis. Cross-component story dependence must be addressed in grouping/validation or reported unresolved, not assumed cured by a bootstrap.

This paired cluster bootstrap is an **approximate candidate**, not an exact finite-sample coverage theorem for nonlinear BLEU/chrF or a few timing sessions. Adopting it requires pre-outcome synthetic coverage tests and a justified sampling target. The six archived years do not justify inference to arbitrary future years. Missing bootstrap denominators or undefined statistics count as unresolved, never discarded draws. Preserve the original numerical rank allowance separately from sampling error: proposed100,000 draws, common seed2026102508, chunks<=256, at most55 one-sided quantities and total additional rank-tail allowance0.001. Lock the exact ranks and handling of zero-tail counts in the implementation before use.

### Rare-cue reference construction and why it does not yet solve feasibility

A sharper marginal procedure must be justified before it sees new outcomes. Here is one explicit conservative reference to assess, **not a newly adopted replacement for historical Q0**. Let D_ci in[-1,1] be the original paired signed cue loss. Let C_c be the applicable denominator count (m_c for numbers, n_c for negation), A_c=sum max(D_ci,0) over that denominator, and M=128 be the predeclared upper bound on eligible component size. Define Y_c=A_c/M and V_c=C_c/M in[0,1]. The unchanged population signed input-weighted loss is bounded above by sum E[A_c]/sum E[C_c]. This allows negative losses to help the point estimate without treating a positive-only envelope as a redefined endpoint.

For J>=2 independent bounded component variables, define r(x,b)=sqrt(2*squared_sample_sd(x)*log(2/b)/J)+7*log(2/b)/(3*(J-1)). Use U_Y=min(1,mean(Y)+r(Y,a/2)), L_V=max(0,mean(V)-r(V,a/2)), and U_cue=min(1,U_Y/L_V) if L_V>0; otherwise unavailable. Here squared_sample_sd uses the ordinary J-1 sample-variance denominator. Union bounding numerator and denominator failures yields a one-sided level-a upper envelope under the independent-component model. Independent nonidentical components are allowed by Maurer-Pontil Theorem11; independence across actual components still needs justification. The ratio derivation is this design's construction, not a claim that the paper validates these news datasets. Apply the same method to all candidate cue endpoints; no method selection from the observed answer. It cannot give a positive lower cue-loss bound to exclude a fixed depth, so retain those unused exclusion slots.

Even with **zero positive losses**, the numerator penalty is7*log(4/a)/(3*(J-1)), while L_V<=C/(J*M), where C is the observed total covered count. A necessary condition to certify margin0.02 is therefore C >= [7*log(4/a)/(3*0.02)]*M*J/(J-1). At a=.025 and M=128, even its strict J->infinity lower limit exceeds75,000 covered inputs. The entire proposed acquisition ceiling is20,000 inputs. Thus this fixed-M reference method **cannot pass within this design**, even under zero observed adverse loss. The [deterministic arithmetic receipt](../../../results/quality_tolerant/population_design/analytic-feasibility.json) records the exact value. Any denominator variance or precision requirement worsens this result. This is a method-specific obstruction, not a universal requirement for every valid cluster method.

Accordingly, a future analytical/synthetic methods audit must either prospectively establish another valid rare-cluster bound with feasible power/precision, or conclude blocked. Do not launch expensive model evaluation with this reference method; do not use an all-zero cluster percentile bootstrap to obtain a zero-width cue guarantee; do not tune M, discard long documents or choose a method because it passes Q1. A source-only observed maximum can be considered only in a separately justified prospective bound with its own population assumptions, not substituted retroactively for the declared population bound128.

## Multiplicity, power/precision and stopping

Retain the locked protected-exclusion logic: one final policy, all-required joint claim at alpha_claim=.025, plus40 simultaneous one-sided exclusion bounds (10 depths x4 endpoints), each at.000625. A fixed depth is excluded only when an adjusted lower quality-loss bound strictly exceeds a margin. No alpha recycling; full always required. Candidate quality and every remaining paired cost contrast must pass; undefined/missing is not a pass. Before confirmation, name the strong comparator as the retained terminal depth with the smallest development mean ready-output time (tie: lower depth), and lock it with the policy; still retain every other potentially feasible fixed control in the required family. Conditional on valid marginal methods, false overall go or any false exclusion is bounded by.05; the additional numerical rank allowance remains separate. The document bootstrap/rare-cue assumptions must be validated before that composition can be used as an empirical guarantee. Two Q1 schedules are hypotheses, not two opportunities to claim final success: choose one causal policy on development and lock it before confirmation; otherwise a new multiplicity design is required.

Retain >=80% planned power, cue alternative p=.005 with p=.01 sensitivity and null/boundary .02 stress; half-margin upper uncertainty<=.25 BLEU/.5 chrF/.01 cues, and latency lower uncertainty<=.025 at an assumed7.5% benefit. Require power for the **whole joint gate**, not80% separately for each endpoint. Synthetic scenario power needs a99% Monte Carlo lower bound>=.80, as in Q0; this does not establish real-population power. Use whole-component correlation, unequal sizes and coverage tied to size in validation; do not divide a sentence-count requirement by a guessed design effect and call it validated.

Required future tests include rare/zero adverse clusters, cue coverage zero/one, all-covered/uncovered components, margin equality, fractional signed losses, unequal component sizes, perfect within-component dependence, size/outcome dependence, shared-story cross-component dependence as a failing-assumption control, protected fixed-depth exclusions, undefined resampling denominators, corpus/direct parity, paired block effects and cap exhaustion. A cluster-method audit must freeze its exact cases, proof obligations and rejection criteria before synthetic execution. Proposed ceiling48,000 synthetic datasets, zero empirical outcomes, zero bootstrap draws in the first cue-only method audit, one execution/no retries; corpus/timing bootstrap validation would need its own explicit allocation. No synthetic experiment is run by this planning work.

Stop before model execution if source eligibility, split isolation, cue coverage, component concentration, independence model, rare-cue validity, precision or joint-power criteria are unverified or fail. Stop a later experiment on parity/hash/access/budget failure; retain its receipt. Confirmation is one-shot after policy/comparator lock; no outcome-based backfill, repeated peeking, sample expansion, fallback schedule or re-fitting. A new protocol can study a different claim, but cannot silently relabel a failure as success.
