## Explicit error-control proof and limits

Let E be any erroneous exclusion of a truly quality-feasible fixed depth. Each such erroneous endpoint lower bound has probability <=.000625 if its marginal procedure is valid. The union bound over40 gives P(E)<=.025, under arbitrary dependence across endpoints/depths.

If overall scientific success is false, either a candidate quality condition is false, full saving is not positive, or at least one truly feasible depth has no positive improvement. In the first two cases, a fixed false component must pass its .025 test for global go. In the last case, choose one truly feasible non-improved depth in the population (not from observed results). Outside E it remains in the comparison, and its false superiority test must pass at .025. Therefore `P(false global go or E)<=.025+.025=.05`. This holds without independence between component tests, provided their marginal error guarantees hold. Removing tests after observing data, choosing multiple candidate winners, unadjusted exclusions or treating undefined values as passes breaks the argument.

This is a proof for the joint decision/exclusion events, not proof of exact BLEU/chrF/bootstrap population coverage. CP guarantees require the stated sampling assumptions; document dependence remains unresolved. Synthetic uniform-p tests validate composition conditional on valid marginal p-values; they are not translations, timings or power estimates for corpus quality. Known-cluster stress tests illustrate assumption failures, not actual news-document correlations. Unknown checkpoint-training overlap and consumed-data selection remain disclosed.
