## Paired inference and sparse cues

Primary cue interval: for paired adverse loss D in [-1,1], U(k,n,a)=1 if k=n else Beta.isf(a,k+1,n-k). Use `[-U(count(D<0),n,a), U(count(D>0),n,a)]`. The deterministic inequality `-P(D<0) <= E[D] <= P(D>0)` justifies this envelope without pretending fractional F1 is Bernoulli. At zero adverse events, U=1-a**(1/n). Undefined numeric coverage is unavailable, never a pass; require at least `ceil(log(a)/log(.98))` covered independent groups even before considering observed adverse events. Negation needs the same count on its all-group denominator. Only a mathematical full-self identity bypasses this; empirical equality of a candidate does not. No bootstrap-only zero interval for rare cues.
