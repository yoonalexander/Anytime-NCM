# Proposed next unit: richer causal-signal pilot

Planning only, produced by the completed controller diagnosis. This document is not an execution protocol or release. The diagnosis ends before new GPU work or fitting. Q0 remains failed, Q4 is not ready, and E3/E4/unallocated outcomes remain sealed.

The diagnosis reproduced the old controller exactly. Its combined calibration scores ranked adverse proxy losses poorly (AUC 0.275 at 8, 0.509 at 10); individual BLEU/chrF rankings were also near 0.5. This supports investigating richer information available at the current observation, rather than widening the same thresholds. It does not prove the 19 raw features lack information or distinguish linear-model failure from sample/target mismatch. All 260 fit negation-change labels were 0 at both levels; richer features alone cannot supply missing positive supervision for that head.

Recommended sequence:

1. Inspect the existing decoder-logit path and add a separate, parity-tested instrumentation adapter. Candidate groups: current top-two token-logit margins and current token-distribution entropy (mean/minimum or lower percentile summaries), plus literal source/current number-preservation comparisons. Everything must be available at 8 or 10using only the actual source/current/past observations. No reference coverage flag, future text/code, IDs or old oracle exit enters runtime features. Do not change original reference-defined numeric F1 or any tolerance. Confidence and literal cues do not certify semantic correctness.
2. Before fitting, run a small measurement/parity pilot on 16 fit-role representatives chosen by canonical metadata order. Use the existing frozen checkpoint; do not collect on the former 87 evaluation inputs or any reservation. Compare the old cheap-feature instrumentation with rich-feature instrumentation under the same 8/10/11 observations, forced 11for this diagnostic, so the additional feature cost is measured. No learned stopping or quality claim follows. Check exact codes and every observation against old fit trajectories; record only compact features/timings/hashes, not full logits or new trajectories.
3. If that engineering pilot passes its prospectively frozen finite-feature/parity/cost checks, separately design the fit/calibration signal-discrimination study. Test whether the richer signals distinguish low-loss refinement opportunities before fitting another controller family. Keep all fixed diagnostics and rare-label counts; any future family selection is development on consumed data. A new data source for rare cues would require its own eligible-population/document/exposure allocation and access release; do not unseal existing reservations.

Suggested pilot envelope to turn into a prospective lock before execution: 16 fit inputs;2 instrumentation methods;2 separate sessions;2 timed repeats;5 warmups per method/session, from the same 16 fit inputs.148 total requests including 20 warmups; <=1,628 generator levels,444 decoders,0 encoders;180 source-prefix calls including 32 prechecks;2 checkpoint loads;16 old trajectory bodies,0 reference bodies;0 fits/targets/new inputs; local cost $0; wall<=3,600seconds, RSS<=16GiB/worker, GPU<=8 GiB and physical limit, artifacts<=64MiB;0 retries. These values are proposed, not consumed work or an approved lock. Exact adapter/tests/budgets must be frozen and pushed first.

Proposed engineering screen: unchanged released model states, exact intermediate/final parity, all values finite, evidence that intended confidence features vary across the pilot, and mean added feature cost<=1 ms plus p95<=3 ms in both observed sessions. These timing limits are conservative design heuristics. They are not statistically justified by 16 inputs, do not guarantee 5% controller savings, and must be reviewed against the final observation schedule. Entropy/top-k extraction has real GPU/transfer cost even though logits already exist.

Any later supervised cue head must report adverse/nonadverse training counts and identify all-zero or otherwise unsupported labels explicitly. A planning floor such as 5 adverse/5 nonadverse examples is only a development sanity check, not power or assurance for the 0.02 margin. Do not present a constant-zero head as learned protection, bypass the original quality screen, or silently change which references define numeric F1.

[CALM (Schuster et al.,2022)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07061) studies confidence measures and local-versus-sequence decisions in a different autoregressive setting. It motivates examining confidence signals; none of its speedups or statistical guarantees transfer to this NCM checkpoint by citation. This pilot would establish instrumentation/parity/cost only. Whether the signals predict useful NCM exits and whether a controller preserves quality while saving actual time remain open.
